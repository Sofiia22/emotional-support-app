# Plekai privacy notice — draft

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Plekai is currently a local-first wellbeing application. It does not connect to an AI service, analytics provider, advertising network, account server, or cloud database.

## Data stored by the app

The current build can store the selected language, local demo profile, mood check-ins, journal entries, breathing-session count, favorite reading items, support region, and reminder preferences. This information is stored on the device using the platform's application storage.

## Data sharing

Plekai does not automatically transmit this information. A user can deliberately open the operating system's share sheet to export journal entries or a JSON copy of local app data. The destination selected in that share sheet is controlled by the user and may have its own privacy practices.

## Notifications and calls

If enabled, reminder notifications are scheduled locally by the device. Plekai asks for confirmation before opening a phone call to an emergency or crisis number. The telephone provider and contacted service may process call information under their own policies.

## Deletion

The settings screen can clear mood history, journal entries, breathing progress, and favorites. Removing the app may also remove local application data according to the operating system's behavior.

## Important release requirement

This draft must be reviewed and updated with the publisher's legal identity, contact address, launch countries, final technical architecture, and store-specific disclosures before public release.
