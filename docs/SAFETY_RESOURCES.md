## Release safeguards

- Re-verify every number and URL shortly before release and on a recurring schedule.
- Have the wording and escalation flow reviewed by a qualified mental-health and safety professional.
- Add launch-region-specific language and accessibility information.
- Never present Plekai as therapy, diagnosis, medical care, or an emergency service.
